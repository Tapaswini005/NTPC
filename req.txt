***Predictive Maintenance System for Power Plant Equipment***
##Project Idea:

Develop a machine learning system that predicts whether a power plant machine/equipment may require maintenance based on operational parameters.

##Objective

Reduce unexpected equipment failures and improve plant efficiency through predictive analysis.

##Features
-Equipment data entry (temperature, pressure, vibration, etc.)
-Data visualization dashboard
-Machine learning prediction
-Maintenance status:
-Normal
-Warning
-Maintenance Required
-Report generation
-Technologies
-Python
-Pandas
-Scikit-learn
-Streamlit
-Excel/CSV Dataset
